PRICES CURRENT.

With the Assize of Bread, for the Cities of New-York and Philadelphia.

Printed for the Publick, from the Prices Current of the Mid-Atlantic Newspapers, 1770–1810.

Concept mockup in a 1770–1790 letterpress style. The wording is illustrative; every price is a real median of the printed prices (US$, ≈ = converted from pounds and shillings), and the bread figures come from real assize notices.
