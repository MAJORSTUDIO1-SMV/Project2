Concept mockup in a trading-terminal style. Prices are real medians of the prices printed in Mid-Atlantic newspapers (US$; many converted from pounds and shillings). Historical price currents have no bids, asks, volume or live ticks: "Last" is the period median, "Chg" compares with the previous period, and "Prints" counts how many distinct printed prices the median comes from.
